# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

* **Principal (P)** = Initial amount of money
* **Rate (R)** = Rate of interest per year
* **Time (T)** = Time period in years

## Example

If:

* Principal = 1000
* Rate = 5%
* Time = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = 100

Therefore, the simple interest is **100**.

## Inputs

The calculator accepts:

1. Principal amount
2. Rate of interest
3. Time period

## Output

The calculator calculates and displays the simple interest based on the values provided by the user.
